01 · Abstract

The coupling

The Clifford torus — the minimal embedding of the Cartesian product of two circles in the unit 3-sphere — is shown to admit a natural harmonic-coupling structure when doubled through the Cayley-Dickson construction. Quaternion and octonion representations define a twin-torus geometry in which resonant phase coherence between the two foliated surfaces is governed by an SU(2) double-cover symmetry, with the Casimir operator fixing the invariant coherence energy across the coupling.

Extending the construction to SO(4) via the Hopf fibration yields an aperiodic-orbit regime in which the coupling remains phase-stable without periodic closure — the geometric signature of the Macachor Absolute: the invariant energy floor at which coherence is preserved regardless of foliation phase. Candidate applications include plasma confinement and space-weather modeling, where non-local phase coupling between toroidal plasma structures is empirically observed but not formally described.
